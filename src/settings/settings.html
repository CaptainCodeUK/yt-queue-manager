Uses your browser's built-in sync, so it only works reliably between devices running the same browser (e.g. Chrome to Chrome, or Edge to Edge) signed into the same account with sync turned on. It won't bridge different browsers (e.g. Chrome to Vivaldi), and support varies across Chromium-based browsers — this may not work as expected.

Manual export/import (experimental)

If sync above isn't an option for you — your devices use different browsers, or you'd rather not enable it — copy your queue below and get the text to your other device however suits you (a note, a message to yourself, a text file), then paste it back in here to import it.
